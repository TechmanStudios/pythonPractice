## If you Go LIVE - try these suggestions to enhance your Subscription plan.


### Create personalized badges to help your subscribers stand out

This badge will appear next to your subscribers' comments whenever you’re LIVE — Think of it as a identifying symbol of your community! You can also design several badge icons to reflect how long your subscribers have been a part of your community to make them feel special.


### Set a Subscription Goal and pin it during your LIVEs

First, set your goal to just one subscriber and decide how you’re going to celebrate when you achieve it. Once your goal is set, viewers can see its progress during your LIVEs. You can also pin the Subscription Goal during your LIVEs to tell your viewers that this is something you're focussed on at the moment.


### Leverage LIVE tools to spread that word about your Subscription

Explain what benefits you'll provide, as well as the Subscription Goal, and encourage your viewers to be among the first to subscribe! Use stickers to direct viewers to tap on the Subscription icon during your LIVEs or use your other platforms to tell people about your Subscription package.


### Add more value to your Subscription package by adding customized perks

Choose from a list of perks provided by TikTok or customize your own that best aligns with you, your expertise, and community! These could include multi-guest requests, choosing LIVE topics, a chance to game together, a Discord role, etc.
