Do search views, follower feed, or For You feed count? 218K views Last updated: Oct 15, 2024 Let's clarify what counts as a qualified video view, so it’s clear as daylight:


## Authentic Views Only

At TikTok, we're all about the real deal! Qualified views exclude anything fishy like fraud, paid views, disliked views, or those quick clicks of less than five seconds.


## Watch Time Matters

We're counting views where folks stick around for at least five seconds. That's when the magic starts!


## One View per Account

We're totally fair game here! Multiple views from the same account are counted as just one unique view.


## No Ads or Promotions

Keep it organic! Ads, paid promotions, and sponsored content don't make the cut for rewards.


## A Note on View Boost Benefit

While views from View Boost Benefit don't count towards the Creator Rewards Program, they're super helpful for growing your audience. So, keep boosting! Stay genuine and keep those views coming!
