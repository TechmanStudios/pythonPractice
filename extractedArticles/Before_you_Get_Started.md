## Promote, Your Visual Walkthrough Guide


### Here's how you can setup your Promote campaign:

From Video From Video Choose the video you want to promote. Tap the 3-dot icon on the bottom right. Select "Promote" from the bottom row of options. From Profile From Profile Head to your profile and tap the 3 dashes on the top right corner. Go to "TikTok Studio" and select "Promote." From LIVE From LIVE Before starting your LIVE, go to the LIVE panel. Select "Promote" to get things rolling. Easy, right? Let's get promoting!


### Here's how you can request permission through the Promote dashboard:

Head over to the "Mine" section of the Promote dashboard – that's your hub for all things promotion! Navigate to the Requests page, where you can manage who can promote your content and who you can promote. Keep track of all your request history and even fire off new ones right from here. Search up creators or accounts that you would like to promote. Got someone special in mind you'd like to promote? Simply search for their account and send them a request to spread the word!


### Ready to give someone else's video or LIVE a boost? Here's how:

Head over to the video or LIVE you want to promote. Choose your promotion objective and make your payment. Request authorization to promote their content. Once you're approved, sit back and watch your promotion work its magic!


### Received a Promote coupon? Here's how to redeem it:

Head over to your Promote dashboard, click Redeem. Choose your promotion pack. Now just apply your coupon! Click Pay and you're good-to-go!
