## Still not getting the engagement you imagined?

It might not be the recommendation system! Content that is ineligible for the FYF can still be discovered in other ways, such as through search tools or by following an account. When a video does not get many views, it may be due to a lack of community engagement rather than being ineligible for the FYF.
