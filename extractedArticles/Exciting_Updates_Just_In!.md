## Unlocking Rewards & Monetization

So, how can you collect rewards for your awesome effects? Let's take a look at the details to make sure you're getting the most out of your creativity!


### Earning Rewards: The Basics

Your earnings will be based on the number of qualified video publishes within the first 90 days of releasing your effect. Qualified video publishes are public videos from eligible regions that feature your effect. Once your effect reaches 100,000 unique public video publishes, you can start collecting rewards! And the best part? You’ll keep earning from that effect for every additional qualified video publish until the end of the 90 day period or until you hit the maximum reward. Reward amounts can vary based on factors like where the videos featuring your effect were published. Keep this in mind as you track your progress and plan your next moves. Also, remember: effects made using TikTok’s app editor aren’t eligible for rewards; only those crafted with the desktop version are.


### Maximum Rewards: Cha-ching!

Each effect has a cap of $14,000 USD and, as an individual creator, you can pocket up to $50,000 USD per month. It's all about maximizing your earning potential and turning your TikTok creations into cash.


## FAQ


### What Counts as a Qualified Effect?

An effect is considered a qualified effect once it reaches 100,000 unique public video publishes within its first 90 days, or when it starts collecting rewards.


### What's a Qualified video publish?

It's when someone posts a video on TikTok using your effect within the first 90 days of you submitting it to Effect House. Only unique qualified video publishes count toward your rewards. That means, if someone posts multiple videos using the same effect in the same day, it will still only count as one qualified publish. So, to break it down: Qualified Publishes:Your effect used in public TikTok videos from eligible regions within its first 90 days. Only unique instances count. Qualified Publishes: Unique Publishes:One video per user per day = one unique publish. ### Can I collect rewards from effects used in private videos?

Nope, sorry! Only effects used in public videos are eligible to collect rewards.


### Can I still collect rewards from effects I've published in the past?

Only effects published after you've been admitted to the program can snag those rewards. But hey, there's always room for new creations to shine!


### Can I collect rewards from mobile effects or effects I created within the TikTok app?

Unfortunately, no. Only Community Effects crafted using the Effect House desktop tool are eligible.


### If I update a published effect after I join this program, can that effect collect rewards?

Sorry, no. Only effects published after you've joined this program can get in on the rewards action.


### Is there a maximum reward per creator?

Yup! The most a single effect can earn is $14,000 USD, and the maximum a single creator can earn per month is $50,000 USD.
