## Top tips for an awesome LIVE

Before you dive into your first LIVE, here are some pro tips:


### Pick the right time

Choose a time that works best for your audience; consider when they're most active.


### Keep it around 30 minutes

While the duration is totally up to you, around 30 minutes is a sweet spot.


### Stay active

Post a video before your LIVE to make it easy for viewers to join via the For You feed.


### Lighting

Good lighting keeps viewers engaged, so set up your lighting before you start.


### Sounds

Make sure your sound is top-notch – no distractions or background noise, please!


### Internet connection

Test your internet connection beforehand for a smooth experience.


### Camera angle

Stick to one camera angle to avoid viewer confusion.


### Plan ahead

Have a general plan for your LIVE, even if you're not scripting every word.


### Engage your audience

Interact with your viewers by responding to comments and questions.


## How To Get Started?

1. Tap the "Create" icon. 2. Swipe to "LIVE" in the navigation. 3. Choose an image and write a catchy title for your stream. 4. Hit "Go LIVE" to start the party! Once you're LIVE, you can tweak settings like flipping the camera, adding effects, filtering comments, and even appointing moderators (up to 20). For more insights and updates on LIVE features, check out the LIVE Center in the TikTok App. Get ready to rock those LIVEs!
