## Finding your analytics

You can accessTikTok analyticsin three ways: TikTok analytics


### In-app

If you're in the TikTok app, just go to yourprofile> tap themenubars > select "TikTok Studio" > find "Analytics"and click "View all." profile menu TikTok Studio Analytics" View all


### TikTok Studio Web

If you're accessing TikTok Studio via the web, just head to the"Analytics"tab on the left side of your screen. "Analytics"


### TikTok Studio App

In the TikTok Studio app, simply click the"Analytics"tab in the bar at the bottom of your screen. "Analytics" Select your analytics date range for the last 7 days, last 28 days, last 60 days, last 365 days, or a custom report. On the web version, you can only customize your reporting window for data as far back as July 17, 2022, so if you need data before that, you'll need to head in-app. You can download analytics data for up to 365 days at a time.

All of this data allows you to better understand your audience —both who they are and what they want— making it easier to create content with your unique community in mind! Here's what you'll find under each tab: both who they are and what they want


### Overview

View your post views, profile views, likes, comments, shares, and estimated rewards over your selected date range in"Overview."You can also look at your"Traffic source"data to find out how your viewers discovered your posts or what"Search queries"led them to your content. ### Content

Looking for even deeper insights on individual posts? You can also easily spot content that's working by checking the"Your top posts"section, which shows your 10 top-performing videos over the period of time you select, ranked according to views, likes, new viewers, and new followers gained. You can tap on each video to learn more about its performance, including retention rate, traffic sources, and viewer information. "Your top posts"


### Viewers

Your "Viewers"page provides a comprehensive overview of your audience, including key viewer metrics, demographics, activity data, and content your viewers watch. ### Followers

The"Followers"tab holds the key to understanding your growing audience. You can explore all kinds of data on who is actually watching your content, such as gender, age, and top locations. This demographic data is based on a number of factors, including information users provide in their profiles. If you're feeling really curious, you can even explore your audience's consumption habits broken down by hours and days to help you understand when they're most active. "Followers"

## Reviewing individual video performance

You can also access individual analytics that break down the performance of each of your videos. Start by looking at your total video views to get an overview of your performance. Then, get a better understanding of your video's lifecycle in the For You feed by checking out how your viewers engaged with your content— like the exact moment when most people liked your video. If you really want to optimize your content performance, pay attention to these key metrics: Retention rate:This is the percentage of your video that users watched. Find dropoff points and determine what might have been the reason for their loss of interest. Check what content you covered during the dropoff point and if you had text or any other elements that could have led to loss of interest. Retention rate: Average watch time:This represents the average duration viewers spent watching your content. This metric is essential for understanding how relevant and engaging your video is to your audience. Average watch time: Watched full video:This is your finish rate or the percentage of people who watched your content until the last few seconds.
