## Finding your perfect niche

To create engaging content on TikTok, you've got to land on a content niche that's authentic to you! It might help to make a list of all the things you're passionate about. Maybe it's your favorite hobby, an aspect of your career, or a subject you can talk to your friends about for hours. Use that list to explore intriguing hashtags and topics and discover communities of people who share the same passions! Once you find a community that resonates with you, you can post content within that niche to connect with like-minded individuals and collaborate with creators. ## Finding the right format

Zeroed in on your niche? Now it's time to figure out what video format works best! Here are a few content creation styles to consider: Here are a few content creation styles to consider:


### Vlogs

Vlogs are like your personal reality show, sharing slices of your life. Whether it’s your morning routine, the day’s meals, or the behind-the-scenes of your creations, it's all about bringing viewers into your world.


### Step-by-step guidance

A step-by-step video format keeps your audience engaged. Use on-screen text to guide them through each step, ensuring everyone can easily follow your process. Pro tip: Try teasing out the finished result at the beginning so viewers want to stick around and learn!


### Trends

Putting your personal twist on trends can keep your content fresh. Mix trends into your content schedule to connect to a broader audience.
