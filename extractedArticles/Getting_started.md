## Framing

Framing is a technique that directs a viewer’s eye to your desired area on a screen. Using lines within a framecreates depth in a shot and draws attention to your subject immediately.You can frame a shot by positioning your subject within, or in front of, a physical structure like a door frame, window, or wall. Framing can also be done with color, texture, and light. creates depth in a shot and draws attention to your subject immediately. Use the rule of thirds or symmetry as framing guides during your content creation.


## Rule of thirds

The rule of thirds is a simple, effective way totransform your content with no experience necessary.People's eyes naturally go to the intersections of a frame. The rule of thirds takes advantage of this by using negative space to create balance and draw viewers' eyes to a subject. transform your content with no experience necessary. How to use this technique: How to use this technique: First, divide the frame into thirds with two vertical lines. Then divide the frame into thirds with two horizontal lines. You will be left with nine boxes in your frame. Place your subject in the left or right third of the frame. Have points of interest positioned at these lines, like a person's face or the center of a flower. If you're shooting a video of a landscape, align the scene to the horizontal lines. Helpful tips: Helpful tips: Your smartphone’s camera app has a thirds grid overlay. Use this when creating your content to easily practice the rule of thirds. ## Symmetry

Symmetry is a film technique that works by creating balance on opposing sides of a frame. This technique is a bit easier to get the hang of than the rule of thirds and caneasily make a shot feel more intentional and structured.Symmetrical shots are visually satisfying and offer a sense of unity. easily make a shot feel more intentional and structured. How to use this technique: How to use this technique: Divide your frame into two halves with one vertical, horizontal, or diagonal line. Place your subject directly in the center of this line. Or place two subjects on equal, opposite sides of the frame to create balance. With two subjects, place them at an equal distance from the camera.
