## Sharing safely

Everyone has a well-being story unique to them. We want to encourage and maintain a supportive environment on our platform for you to meaningfully share your experiences, tips, and resources.Here are some helpful do's and don'ts to keep in mind as you create your content: Here are some helpful do's and don'ts to keep in mind as you create your content:


### Do:

Be upfront about your professional background, credentials, and training. Not an expert? Consider collaborating with a trusted source. Share your personal experiences when comfortable and provide context. Encourage people who are struggling to reach out to professionals. Look for ways to share your story authentically while offering a message of hope to viewers. Consider your audience and if you want your content to be public or private. You can learn more about the differences between a public and private account in ourPrivacy Center. Privacy Center Privacy Center


### Don't:

Diagnose others or share general information that might encourage self-diagnosis. Give specific medical advice or encourage or discourage specific treatments or medications. Be dismissive of someone's experiences and feelings. Use stigmatizing or graphic language, images, or content. Review ourCommunity Guidelinesfor more on TikTok's best practices. ### Audience controls

If your content may not be suitable for teens — for example, if it contains sensitive themes, go to your "Settings" and turn on "Audience Controls" to ensure your content is only visible to users 18 years or older.
