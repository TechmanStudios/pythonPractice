## Hate Speech and Hateful Behavior

Our platform is enriched by the diversity of our community. This is why content that attacks a person or group because of protected attributes — including race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, and more — is not welcome on TikTok and can lead to suspensions and bans.


## Harassment and Bullying

We seek to create an environment where everyone feels comfortable expressing themselves just as they are. To support a safe and welcoming environment for our community, we do not allow language or behavior that harasses, humiliates, threatens, or doxxes anyone.


## Sensitive and Mature Themes

TikTok provides content that ranges from very family-friendly to more mature. Given the diversity of our global community, developmental and cultural considerations inform our approach to potentially sensitive and more mature content that may be considered offensive by some. ## Safety & Civility


### Sexual Exploitation and Gender-Based Violence

We do not allow sexual exploitation or gender-based violence, including non-consensual sexual acts, image-based sexual abuse, sextortion, physical abuse, and sexual harassment. We are committed to providing a space that embraces gender equity, supports healthy relationships, and respects intimate privacy. Undermining these values can cause trauma and may lead to physical and psychological harm. ## Shocking and Graphic Content

Part of the joy of TikTok is coming across new and unexpected content. But the platform is not a place to intentionally shock, upset, or disgust others. We prioritize the safety and comfort of our community by not allowing gory, gruesome, disturbing, or extremely violent content.


## Youth Safety and Well-Being

We are deeply committed to keeping youth safe on TikTok. We do not allow content that may put young people at risk of exploitation, or psychological, physical, or developmental harm. This includes child sexual abuse (CSAM) and youth abuse, which are strictly not allowed. We report incidents of youth sexual exploitation and abuse to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC). We also report to relevant law enforcement authorities when there is a specific, credible, and imminent threat to a young person's life.


## Unoriginal Content and QR Codes

The creativity on TikTok is what makes it great, so you should only post your own work. We do not allow content that violates someone else's intellectual property rights. If we become aware of content that is a violation, we will remove it. ## Notice and Appeals

In keeping with our commitment to ensuring procedural fairness, we seek to provide notifications to community members if they violate our rules. If you have posted a video, comment, audio, or live-streamed content that we do not allow, you will be notified in the app along with the violation reason. If your account has been banned because of a violation, you will receive a banner notification when you open the app, informing you of this account change. If you receive a notification of a content violation or account ban and believe that it was done in error, then you can appeal the decision.
