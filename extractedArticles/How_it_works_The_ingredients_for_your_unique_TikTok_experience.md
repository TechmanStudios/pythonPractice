## How it works: The ingredients for your unique TikTok experience

The For You feed (FYF) is what makes TikTok unique, delivering all kinds of high-quality content tailored to your specific interests. Meaning, if you're glued to those videos of stand-up comedians working the crowd until the very end, your FYF will get filled with more stand-ups working the crowd. Or when you swipe past an ASMR video without a thought, the less ASMR content you'll get on your feed.
