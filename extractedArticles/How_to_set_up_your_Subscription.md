Now, your most engaged followers can show their love and support for you and your content by subscribing to your community. Through a monthly Subscription, they’ll be able to unlock awesome perks like exclusive videos, LIVEs, threads, badges and more. What’s in it for you? A chance to foster deeper connections, build a more engaged audience, and develop a more reliable monthly revenue — can’t get better than that! Ready to take your community to new heights? Follow these easy steps:


## How to set up your Subscription


### 1. Find Subscription

Head to the TikTok Studio and click on the Subscription button. Or, find it on the Go LIVE page.


### 2. Check eligibility

Once you’re there, you’ll see if you’re eligible to join.


### 3. Click “Join Subscription”

Just tap the button and you’re signed up. Once you’ve joined, users will see an entry on your profile page as well as in LIVE rooms, and when they click on either, they’ll find a benefits page showing all the perks you offer.


### 4. Start with a lower price tier

Next, you'll need to select a price for your Subscription that best reflects your benefits. Prices are set on a monthly subscription basis ranging from $2.99 to $99.99 USD per month. Given it's your first Subscription package, we recommend choosing the lowest recommended price tier to start and seeing how its received by your followers — you can always adjust it later! Remember, your subscribers will pay in their local currency, so keep those exchange rates and taxes in mind when setting your price tier.


### 5. Set up your perks

You can set up perks immediately after joining or at any time after that in the management page. Choose from a list of perks provided by TikTok or customize your own that best aligns with you, your expertise, and community! These could include multi-guest requests, choosing LIVE topics, a chance to game together, a Discord role, etc. Just don't forget to keep it realistic — only promise what you can deliver.


## What kind of perks should you offer?

People love to feel like they’re part of a special club and the content you share in your Subscription can help create the vibe that best aligns with you, your expertise, and community including:


### Exclusive content

Offer sub-only videos. These are videos that only subscribers can see (non-subscribers will only see the video cover). Use this feature to post more personal content, share behind-the-scenes footage, or publish early drafts of videos that aren't ready for the public yet. Offer sub-only videos


### Exclusive chats/interactions

Use your sub-only space.Exclusively available to your subscribers, this forum-like feature enables you to post topics and create polls for discussion threads. Pose questions or start conversations about upcoming videos, brainstorm new content ideas, seek feedback, and encourage participation to increase engagement. Use your sub-only space.


### Badges and stickers

Upload up to 35 personalized stickersfor your Subscription community to use during LIVEs or within Direct Messages. Sub-only stickers are custom emoticons unique to your community that only your subscribers can send in DMs and LIVE chats. Emotes such as happy, sad, excited, pictures of yourself with text, or even inside jokes you have with your followers are a great start! Upload up to 35 personalized stickers Create personalized badges to help your subscribers stand out.This badge will appear next to your subscribers' comments whenever you’re LIVE — think of it as a identifying symbol of your community! You can also design several badge icons to reflect how long your subscribers have been a part of your community to make them feel special. Create personalized badges to help your subscribers stand out.
