## Intro to TikTok Moderation

At TikTok, our mission is toinspire creativity and bring joy. Bringing our mission to life requires that we remove harmful content and accounts to provide our community with a supportive environment. In this article, we'll dive into how we moderate content and provide transparency into how we work to keep everyone safe on TikTok. inspire creativity and bring joy


## Safety Checks

We're focused on fostering a safe and welcoming space for you to be able to share your creativity and connect with others. OurCommunity Guidelinesoutline the rules for being on TikTok, and we use a combination of technology and moderators to enforce our policies. Community Guidelines Community Guidelines We use multiple safety checks to help us identify and remove content that breaks our rules on TikTok. The first safety check happens when a video is uploaded. All content uploaded to TikTok initially passes through technology that scans the video for potential policy violations. Our technology will remove content if it has policy violations. Any uncertainties about a potential violation are sent to a safety team member for further review. If a violation is found, the video will be removed. If no violation is identified, the video will be posted on TikTok. It's important to note that views may vary from video to video, and some content is not eligible for recommendation because it not suitable for the broad audience of the For You feed. Another safety check happens when a video reaches a certain threshold of views. In order to reduce the risk of harmful content gaining popularity, our automated moderation systems may send videos with a high number of views to our content moderators for further review against our Community Guidelines. We also review content that's been reported by our community.


## Our Account Enforcement System

We've developed a system to provide you with a clear and consistent moderation experience. If you post content that violates our policies, the content will be removed and your account will accrue a strike. If your account meets the threshold of strikes within either a product feature (i.e. Comments, LIVE) or policy (i.e. Harassment and Bullying), it will be permanently banned. We recognize that certain policy violations pose a greater risk to our community than others. Because of this, the policy thresholds for a permanent ban can vary. For instance, we place greater weight on violations of our hateful ideologies policy than for violations of our spam policy. We still maintain a zero-tolerance policy for areas such as threatening violence or showing torture — and we'll issue permanent bans on the first strike for accounts that engage in these behaviors. Additionally, accounts that accrue a high number of cumulative strikes across policies and features may be banned from the platform. We'll notify you if you're on the verge of having your account permanently removed. Our goal with this system is to help us remove harmful accounts and repeat offenders quickly and efficiently.We know that most people on TikTok don't want to violate our policies, but may just need reminders from time to time about our rules. We do believe that growth is possible, so strikes will expire from your account record after 90 days. Our goal with this system is to help us remove harmful accounts and repeat offenders quickly and efficiently.


## Appeals

When a video, audio, comment, or LIVE violates our policy, we remove the content and notify you of the reason. In most cases, you'll be given the option to appeal. We encourage everyone to appeal moderation decisions they believe to have been made in error. To appeal, tapAppealin the upper right corner of the moderation result notification page and submit the request. After you do so, a member of our safety team will review your report per our Community Guidelines. We'll notify you of the outcome of your appeal. If your appeal is successful, the content will be restored, and the penalty will be removed from your account. Appeal
