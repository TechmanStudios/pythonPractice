# Is Creator Rewards Program available for all account types?

Monetize Other frequently asked questions Other frequently asked questions 177K views Last updated: Oct 15, 2024 Are you totally psyched to explore the exciting world of the Creator Rewards Program? Let's dive into some FAQs and find out everything you need to know about this awesome opportunity!


## Is Creator Rewards Program available for all account types?

Unfortunately, not everyone can join the fun. Business accounts and accounts linked to political or government organizations are not eligible for the Creator Rewards Program. You can check your account's status in your settings.


## How do I confirm I'm 18 or older?

Your age is determined by the birthdate you provided when you signed up for TikTok. If you're applying for the Creator Rewards Program, be prepared to verify your age with a government-issued ID.


## Where can I view my reward estimate?

Keep an eye on your Creator Rewards Program Dashboard to see your estimated rewards. They usually update within three days after you post a video. You can also check your Monthly Earnings Dashboard on the first day of each month.


## Will TikTok review my video for rewards?

Absolutely! TikTok will review your video to see if it's eligible for rewards under the Creator Rewards Program. If it passes, you'll be rewarded based on your content's performance.


## How do I redeem estimated rewards?

You can redeem your rewards on the 15th of each month if they meet the $50.00 USD minimum payment threshold. Make sure you've completed all the onboarding requirements, including providing tax information, to cash in.


## Can I still participate in TikTok Creator Marketplace?

Yes! You can still partner with brands and explore monetization opportunities through TikTok Creator Marketplace, even if you're part of the Creator Rewards Program.


## What happened to Creativity Program Beta? And how do I switch to the Creator Rewards Program from Creativity Program Beta?

Many of you have been creating longer videos and enjoying the benefits of Creativity Program Beta. But the clue has been in the name: Creativity Program Beta. We’ve been listening closely to all the feedback TikTok creators like you have given. So the Creativity Program left beta and became the Creator Rewards Program. If you were already enrolled in Creativity Program Beta, no action is required, and you will automatically be enrolled in the Creator Rewards Program. Now, wasn't that easy!


## What's the difference between Creator Rewards Program and Creator Fund?

While both programs reward creators based on video performance, there are a few key differences. In the Creator Rewards Program, videos must be at least one minute long to qualify for rewards, and there's a higher incentive for qualified video views. Plus, you'll get to see your estimated rewards on a Dashboard!


## How do I join/switch to the Creator Rewards Program?

Creators already part of the Creativity Program Beta will automatically be enrolled in the Creator Rewards Program. Creators who wish to join the Creator Rewards Program can go to their Profile > Menu (☰) > Settings > TikTok Studio > Creator Rewards Program to apply.
