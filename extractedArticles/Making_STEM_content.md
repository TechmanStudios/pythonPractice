## Making STEM content

To have your content featured on the STEM feed, here's what you need to do: Make a STEM-related video around any STEM topic of your choice. Include a complete lesson that answers all the questions. For example, if you're talking about traveling to the Moon, don't just ask viewers how far it is; make sure to include the actual distance. Keep your content appropriate for ALL viewers aged 13 and above and adhere to ourCommunity Guidelines. STEM topics intended for younger users (under 13), such as elementary lessons on basic shapes or colors, are not appropriate for our STEM feed.
