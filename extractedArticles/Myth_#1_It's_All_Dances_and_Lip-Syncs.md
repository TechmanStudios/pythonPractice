## Different Ways to Showcase Your Music on TikTok

Now that we've cleared up some myths, let's discuss how you can use your own voice to shine as an artist on TikTok.


## Explore Music Genres, Trends, and More Content Strategy

Part of finding your artistic voice on TikTok is via exploration: Don't be afraid to experiment and try genres, performances, and video styles that feel authentic to you. And it's fine if you don't know right off the bat what exactly those might be! TikTok is a community where everyone belongs — it's an inclusive platform that encourages you to be your authentic self. Here are some ideas to help you pinpoint your video taste on TikTok.


## Connect With Others

Think beyond music! TikTok lets you follow your passions and find new fans in those communities. Furthermore, you can follow other artists on TikTok and get inspiration from them, as well as from popular music hashtags in the community. Our hashtags are designed to give you a natural route into creation. You can also use TikTok features, such asDuetsandStitches, that allow you to showcase your musical ability with other creators. Add a verse, instrument, or harmonize with other artists, or put your personal musical spin on the latest TikTok trend. Duets Duets Stitches Stitches


## Pinpoint Your Performance Style

Discover what kind of performance videos you want to make — do you like acoustic performances? Do you prefer a capella? Song covers? Is there a scenic spot you can record from? Consider all these things for your videos, and you can also ask your audience to duet with your performances, as well! And if you want a longer performance on TikTok, don't forget that you can go LIVE!


## Get Personal in Your Videos

And lastly, storytelling is very powerful — what do you want your fans to learn about you? Tell your audience your story in addition to sharing your music. Use the Add Yours feature, which allows you to interact with others on TikTok through a prompt that you can add to your video or respond to.
