# Navigating to Your Profile

## Navigating to Your Profile

In the TikTok app, tap your"profile"button at the bottom right to see your profile page. This page is where you can see your published TikTok content, drafts, bio, profile picture, and more. You can see videos you've liked as well as what you've Favorited, and you can adapt your privacy settings if you wish to make these private. "profile"


## Personalize Your Profile

You can tap"Edit profile"to begin customizing your page and ensuring it aligns with your personal brand. Here are some ways you can optimize your profile to fit who you are and what kind of content you create: "Edit profile"


### Profile Picture

The circular image up top is the icon users will see when you post, like, or comment. This image shows who you are, so make sure to choose something that represents you! You can switch your image, or add a new one, by tapping the camera icon.


### Username

Below the profile picture, you'll see what your current TikTok username is. You can tap the pen icon to edit it. You're permitted to change your username once every 30 days. Changing your username will also change your profile link.


### Name

Your name is what will appear (rather than your username) when you like, comment, and post.


### Bio

You have 80 characters to tell users who you are and what kind of content you make.


### Pronouns

Select your pronouns, which will be visible on your page, so users know how to refer to you.


### Organization

Below your bio is the Nonprofit section, where you can select which organization you'd like to support in your profile. When you select one, a Donation sticker will appear on your page, where users can tap to donate to the organization you selected.


### Links

After that is the Links section. You can include your links to other platforms so your audience can keep up with you off TikTok.


### Add Yours

Add Yours allows you to interact with others on TikTok through a prompt that you can add to your video or respond to. Learn more about it atTikTok Support! TikTok Support TikTok Support


### Change Display Order

You can swap the order of your Social pages and the Add Yours feature, giving you the ability to customize your page even further.
