## Photosensitive Epilepsy Warning

Beingmindfulof those prone to photosensitive epilepsy makes it possible for those viewers to watch your content. That's where the photosensitive epilepsy warning comes in handy. This warning notifies you when you work with effects that may trigger photosensitive epilepsy. You may see this notification when you select certain effects during the video editing process. If you see this warning, consider whether you want to proceed with such an effect or explore other options. mindful If you yourself have photosensitive epilepsy, you can opt out of viewing triggering content by tapping: Settings & Privacy Settings & Privacy Display Display Remove photosensitive videos toggle Remove photosensitive videos toggle


## Text-to-Speech

One feature that can be particularly helpful to the visually impaired or blind communities is Text-to-speech. This feature allows you to convert typed text to a voice-over that plays over the text as it appears in the video. Text-to-speech can be found on the video editing page and can be enabled by holding down on the text until the "Text-to-speech" prompt appears.


## Auto-Captions and Text on Videos

TheAuto-captions featureautomatically generates subtitles, allowing viewers to read along with your video content. This feature helps viewers who are deaf or hard of hearing enjoy your content. You can select Auto-captions on the editing page after you've uploaded or recorded a video, and then the text is automatically transcribed and displayed on your videos. You may also consider creating intro cards to let the audience know what they're about to see.
