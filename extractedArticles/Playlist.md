## Playlist

Playlists allow you to organize your videos into categorized sets of videos. People can find your Playlists from yourprofile pageor theFor Youfeed. profile page For You feed Videos that are part of a Playlist will feature a link to the full Playlist below the video. Playlists provide users with an ideal viewing experience, where they can continue to watch content on the same topic by the same creator. Therefore, we suggest that you ensure that the videos within the Playlist all have a similar theme and that you describe the videos' theme in the Playlist name. For example, if you're a fitness creator, you can create a Playlist for different leg workouts. Or if you're a beauty creator, you can create Playlists for Get Ready With Me videos, hauls, product reviews, and more. The options are endless, making it easy for both you and your viewers to enjoy Playlists and explore the content you love. If a viewer is interested in your Playlist, they can click the link to enter theDetailspage for more videos from your Playlist. After tapping a video in a Playlist, viewers can swipe through videos, just like in theirFor You feeds, to watch more videos from the Playlist. Details For You feeds

## Add Videos to Your Playlist

You can add videos to an existing Playlist or a new one. A video can only be added to one Playlist and up to 100 videos can be added to a Playlist at one time. There is no limit to the number of videos that a Playlist can hold. The default video order in the Playlist is from oldest to newest according to the time the video was added to the Playlist; you can edit the order of your Playlist. The main way to add videos to a Playlist is mainly through the videoPublishing page,Personal page, and within the video (long press on the panel). Publishing page Personal page
