## Political Ads

We want TikTok to be apolitics-free zonewhen it comes toadvertising. Whether it's paid ads or branded content,political content isn't allowed. So, if you're getting paid to create anything political, you're breaking our rules, and that could lead to actions against your account, even a permanent ban.
