## Search: The Basics

The Search experience allows you to explore videos, other creators, sounds, and more from all over TikTok. You can access Search from the magnifying glass icon on your Home page, or by tapping on a hashtag or search recommendation. Key features include: Search results: Search results are separated into a series of categories, such as Top, Videos, Users, Sounds, LIVE, and Hashtags. You can explore results across these categories for each Search query. Videos in search results are sorted according to a combination of factors, including relevance to your search query and engagement. Relevance is determined based on things like video captions, video text, and hashtags. Search results Search recommendations:Some videos may feature Search recommendations, which appear at the top of a video's comments section, below a video, the Video Detail page search bar, You May Like section, or highlighted within comments. With just one tap, you can explore topics related to a video and discover new content. Search recommendations are generated based on a variety of factors, such as the content of comments and common searches made after watching a video. By exploring recommendations, you can easily find more content that matches your interests and preferences. Some people may see different recommendations for the same video. Search recommendations: Search filters:Search Filters help you refine your Search results. For example, you can sort by relevance, popularity, or recency, or even filter by creators you follow or have interacted with in the past. Think of filters as your Search assistant, streamlining discovery and helping you find what you need. Search filters:

## Safety by Design

Safety is built into the Search experience by design. Here are some of the ways we build safety into the Search experience: Community Guidelines apply to Search:We aim to recommend useful and engaging content while removing results that violate ourCommunity Guidelines. For instance, if a Search is made about something potentially harmful or violative of our Community Guidelines, you will be redirected to our policies for a refresher on what is and isn't allowed on TikTok. Autocomplete is not an option for these kinds of queries and will not be suggested to the community. Community Guidelines apply to Search: Community Guidelines Community Guidelines Reporting: If you're scrolling through Search results and come across content you believe may violate our policies, we encourage you to long-press the video to report it or share feedback. You can also report a Search recommendation.
