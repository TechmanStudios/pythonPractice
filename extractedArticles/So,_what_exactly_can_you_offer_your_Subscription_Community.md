## FAQs


### Why change LIVE Subscription? Why introduce Subscription?

We've seen how LIVE Subscription has impacted creators' lives, empowering them to form deeper connections with their community while also boosting their monthly earnings. That's why we've redesigned the feature to make Subscription available to all eligible creators on TikTok!


### How does this differ from Series?

Subscription lets your most engaged followers show you their support with a monthly subscription to unlock access to exclusive perks. Series, on the other hand, is a collection of videos posted behind a paywall which can be purchased once for access.


### How much will I earn from my Subscription?

After app store fees, TikTok splits the revenue up to 50/50 with you.


### How do I receive rewards from Subscription?

Your monthly rewards will be reflected on your account. Just set up your payment method, and you're good to go!


### How do I manage my Subscription once I've joined?

Managing your Subscription is a breeze. Simply head over to your profile in-app > Menu (☰) > Settings > TikTok Studio > Subscription Management to customize settings and add perks.


### Any rules for subscriber-only content?

Yes! Subscriber-only content cannot include duets, stitches, or sticker creations. Also, exclusive videos can only use commercial sounds. As with all content on TikTok, subscriber-only content must adhere to ourTerms of ServicesandCommunity Guidelines. Terms of Services Terms of Services Community Guidelines Community Guidelines


### Can your Subscription Community cancel their subscriptions at any time?

Subscription Community members can cancel at anytime. Subscription is all about giving them the ease and flexibility to support the creators and content they love, on their own terms. *TikTok reserves the right to have the final say when it comes to setting Subscription prices.
