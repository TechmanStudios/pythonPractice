# What Are Security Alerts?

Community Guidelines Security FAQs Security FAQs 218K views Last updated: Oct 23, 2024 The security of our community is of utmost value to us and we take a number of measures to help protect you on TikTok. And while we continuously work to keep you safe, we also want to empower our community to adopt good security habits in order to better protect their accounts. Below are some steps you can take to better secure your account.


## What Are Security Alerts?

Security alerts are alerts that may appear in your security alerts tab when you use the TikTok app or access TikTok through a web browser. They can also be sent via email, SMS, and/or push notifications. These alerts may indicate suspicious behavior, such as a recent password change, a new device being used, or additional security related updates and alerts. You can monitor recent unusual security events using the "security alerts" tab, which can be found within the security tab in your account settings.


## How Do I Create a Secure Password?

A safe experience on TikTok begins with a secure password. When creating a password, think about using a password that's memorable for you but difficult to guess for others. Strong passwords are usually at least 12 characters long and contain a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols. Be sure to set up strong and unique password/PINs for all devices you use to access TikTok, such as laptops, phones, and other mobile devices. A password manager can help take the guesswork out of remembering dozens of unique usernames and passwords.


## What Is Two-Step Verification and How Can I Enable It?

Adding two-step verification is an easy way to safeguard your account. In the event your password has been compromised, two-step verification provides an extra layer of security to help protect your account from unauthorized access by third-party devices or applications. To enable two-step verification, first, go to your profile and select the "…" icon. From there, simply select the security tab and change the two-step verification to "on."


## How Do I Avoid Phishing Attacks?

Phishing attacks occur when someone is tricked into giving up any of their personal information, including passwords, credit card numbers, or other sensitive data. We most commonly see these as emailed phishing attacks targeting creators. Warning signs of phishing can also commonly include promises for verified badges on TikTok. We recommend being cautious with email requests for any the information described above as they could be phishing attacks attempting to gain access to your TikTok login information, or login information for other apps and services you use.


## How Do I Verify My Device?

You can view phones and other mobile devices that are currently logged in or that have accessed your account recently by visiting the "Manage devices" tab under your security settings. This will display all devices associated with your account, how you logged into the account, and when. Check this information to make sure no one else has signed into your account, and remove any devices you do not recognize.
