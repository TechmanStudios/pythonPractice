## Why Branded Content?

Brands ask for branded content for a number of reasons. The message brands want to get across can seem more authentic coming from a creator rather than the brand itself. Brands also gain the ability to raise awareness and visibility within a desirable demographic. When you choose to make a branded post, you are leveraging your followers and audience — an audience that a brand may not otherwise have access to — in exchange for compensation from the brand. This type of audience engagement is a benefit to both you and brands alike. It allows you to not only earn funds, but also explore creating different types of content you may not have considered otherwise, while also helping brands attract potential new customers.

## TikTok Creator Marketplace

Creator Marketplaceis the official collaboration platform to connect brands and creators on TikTok. Creators that participate in Creator Marketplace have the opportunity to connect and collaborate with brands on paid campaigns. Creator Marketplace creators can disclose branded content and apply the "Paid partnership" label by going to Content disclosure settings, toggling on Disclose video content, and tapping "Branded content."
