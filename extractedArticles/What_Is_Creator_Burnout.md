## What Is Creator Burnout?

First of all, we want to validate your feelings when it comes to creating on TikTok, and want to assure you that creator burnout is a very real thing. And that's not just coming from us — theWorld Health Organizationagrees: burnout is real. So what is it? World Health Organization World Health Organization The International Classification of Diseases (ICD-11) defines it as: a syndrome conceptualized as resulting from chronic workplace stress that has not been successfully managed. It is characterized by three dimensions: Feelings of energy depletion or exhaustion Increased mental distance from one's job, or feelings of negativism or cynicism related to one's job Reduced professional efficacy


## What Can Burnout Look Like?

Burnout— which is specifically related to work stress — can manifest through emotional or physical symptoms, interpersonal symptoms, or professional symptoms. Emotional and physical symptoms can include: chronic fatigue, appetite changes, and insomnia. Compassion fatigue, disconnection, and isolation are examples of interpersonal symptoms. And lack of motivation, purposelessness, and performance issues can be signs of professional burnout symptoms. Burnout


## Ways to Prevent Burnout

Before you reach burnout, you can reassess how your symptoms are developing and try to course-correct. For example, you could: delegate tasks; take time off; set thoughtful boundaries; reconnect with your friends and family; or seek support from a professional. Furthermore, another step for burnout prevention is being kind to yourself through self-compassion. Give yourself the space to understand your experience and emotions and take time to seek what you need, whether that's rest, connection, or recognition. Even if you aren't feeling symptoms of creator burnout coming on, it's a good idea to assess how you're feeling about your posting cadence, engagement, and schedule. Are you setting goals for yourself that feel achievable given your current bandwidth (energy, time, emotional capacity)? How do youfeelabout the amount of videos you post per week, and if you are increasing this cadence, how do you foresee that changing your stress level? If you need to take a step back from posting, you should definitely take that break. The number of videos and frequency that you post don’t impact yourcontent's performance, so we encourage you to take a break if and when you're feeling burned out. ## How to Prioritize Your Mental Health

Taking a step back to address stress or burnout and prioritizing your mental health can take shape in many different ways. Consider caring for your body (eating, sleeping, drinking water, moving); caring for your mind (thought patterns, emotions); and establishing a routine (healthy habits, structure). You can also prioritize your mental health by asserting professional and personal boundaries, which may also contribute to mounting stress in your life.
