## What Is Creator Marketplace?

Creator Marketplaceis the official collaboration platform to connect brands and creators on TikTok. With Creator Marketplace, you can collaborate with brands on both paid and reward-based campaigns. Creator Marketplace


## What Are the Benefits of Joining?

When you participate inCreator Marketplace, you will have the opportunity to connect with brands and sponsorship opportunities. Other benefits include getting official support from TikTok on brand campaigns, receiving access to online collaboration tools (such as Open Applications, and TikTok Creator Marketplace Pay), as well as tips on creating advertiser-friendly videos for brands. ## Who Is Eligible?

Once you meet theeligibility requirements(based on factors like age, followers, video content, and engagement), you may be invited to join or apply. You must also be using a Personal (or Creator) account; Business accounts are not eligible at this time. Creator Marketplace is currently available in 24 countries/regions. eligibility requirements When you apply, your application will be reviewed and a notification will be sent with your application status. If you do not pass the initial review, you can reapply after 30 days.


## How Does the Creator Marketplace Work?

After joining Creator Marketplace, go toTikTok Studiothen tap "Monetization." Tap "profile" to manage your creator profile. Best practices include filling in your profile completely and setting a starting rate for sponsored videos (this can be further negotiated on a campaign-by-campaign basis). TikTok Studio Monetization profile When brands send you an invitation to collaborate, you will be notified with an in-app notification, email, and SMS. Creator Marketplace notifications can be found under "Creator Monetization," and all notifications will always be at the top of your inbox.


## Working With Brands

Creator Marketplaceallows brands to search for creators just like you.Brandswill have the ability to see your profile, audience demographics, engagement metrics, and more. Creator Marketplace Brands Best practices suggest aligning with the brand on the video details, including the shooting location, wardrobe, number of reshoots, and payment details. Remember to disclose your Branded content video using the Content disclosure setting and link your video to your Creator Marketplace campaign. This will help brands access real-time data about the campaign including reach and impact. Once you upload your video, it will be reviewed to ensure it meets TikTok's Community Guidelines and branded content policy. You'll receive a notification in your TikTok app if it is approved or rejected. If it's denied, you can reshoot and re-upload the video.
