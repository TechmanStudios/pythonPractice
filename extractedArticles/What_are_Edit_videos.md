### Compilation

Showcase a series of clips or images that share a common theme. For example, the cast of a trending TV show in their first role and now.


### POV

Take a specific perspective and back it up with footage to support your idea. For example, you might say something like "This movie teaches us about the power of friendship" and back that statement up with clips or images from the film.


### Plot

Anything from simple, quick Edits with some traces of editing and a complete storyline (think following one couple's relationship throughout a season or series) to more complicated versions combining the plots or characters into a new storyline (think "What if these enemies became lovers?").


### Dialogue

Select dialogue clips from films and TV shows that highlight a specific character or emotion from one film or show, or include a collection of lines from a variety of films or shows that all have a similar theme! For example, "The 5 saddest breakup lines in movies."


### Character

Mash up videos of your favorite stars or characters with different fun themes, like looks from different time periods or the hottest TV dads.


### Emotion

Combine footage, lines of dialogue, music, and more to express an "emotional" theme like yearning, love, jealousy, etc. Think about your classic "ship" edits here!


### Aesthetic

Highlight a film's aesthetics through clips of original film scenes and background music that enhance the vibe, providing an immersive experience for your audience.


### Creative

Show off your editing skills using advanced effects to create an artistic and impressive new piece of content.


### Mood

Combine a high volume of footage with a piece of music and then edit it according to the music's beats or rhythm.
