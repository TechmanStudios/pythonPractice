## What are sub-only videos?

These are videos that only subscribers can see (non-subscribers will only see the video cover). Use this feature to post more personal content, share behind-the-scenes footage, or publish early drafts of videos that aren't ready for the public yet. Each video has its own cover which will display on your profile page, so make sure it's equally creative as it is enticing to encourage/attract non-subscribers to hit that subscribe button and become the newest members of your community! Your Subscription benefits page also showcases your sub-only videos, offering anyone curious about your content or ready to subscribe a glimpse into the perks your subscribers get to enjoy..


## How to post sub-only videos

You can post sub-only videos in one of two ways. You can either set the visibility to "Subscribers can view this post" when posting, or post directly from the sub-only video tool on your Subscription management page. If you want to add music to your sub-only videos make sure to use songs from our Commercial Music Library. This ensures your videos can be marked as "sub-only." Using music outside the CML will prevent your videos from being eligible to be "sub-only."
