This is the most basic format style involving a simple explanation of the plot of a film or TV show in your own words. Once you’ve gathered your authorized clips (you always want to respect intellectual property rights when creating fan content!), simply break down the plot in a straightforward, easy-to-follow way using those clips, images, and text-on-screen to help your audience follow along. ### Musical Recap

If you're feeling a bit more creative, musical recaps really help your fan content sing! This involves singing, rapping, or otherwise musically retelling the synopsis — think reimagining your favorite comedy series as a Broadway musical. You might even consider adding a costume or prop to stand out even more.


### Character POV

This is your chance to give a supporting character the "main character" treatment. Put a new and creative spin on the plot of a film or TV show by telling it through the perspective of one of the side characters. If you're always wondering how that barista just trying to get through the work day reacted when a giant monster stormed through New York City, this is for you!
