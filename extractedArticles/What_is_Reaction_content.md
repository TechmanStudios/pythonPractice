## What is Reaction content?

Reaction videos have always been a big hit on TikTok, but with the rising popularity of fan content on the platform, they’re quickly becoming fan favorites. This format involves putting you on camera, watching the buzziest film and TV trailers, clips, and more, and giving your immediate, in-the-moment response, whatever that may be! ### “Emotional” reactions

Imagine your most beloved will-they-won’t-they couple just kissed for the very first time…that’s the kind of moment we want to capture in “emotional” reaction videos. Include every squeal, giggle, and scream, so your audience can see and feel everything you’re experiencing in real-time.


### “Critique” reactions

Channel your inner film critic and look at trending entertainment with a more discerning eye in “critique” reaction videos. This type of Reaction video feels a bit more professional, allowing you to use your unique expertise or knowledge to inform your opinions. How do the special effects look? Is the casting everything you imagined? Is there something you would have done differently? Your audience wants to know what you think, so let them in on your thought process every step of the way.


### “Challenge” reactions

If simply watching something isn’t enough to get you pumped, this style ups the entertainment value by adding in an interactive element. Incorporating activities such as the “try-not-to-laugh” or “blindfold” challenge, these reactions help you unlock a new level of fun and engagement.
