## What is add link to video?

As a Subscription creator, you can add a link to any video to lead viewers to your Subscription introduction page. This video is your opportunity to introduce your Subscription and entice viewers to join! It’s the perfect place to show off the benefits and perks you're offering, your future content plans, and any new updates to your Subscription. Depending on their subscriber status, clicking on the in-video link will lead viewers to your Subscription detail page (encouraging them to join) or the subscriber benefits page (if they’ve already joined). On your Subscription detail page, viewers can check out your your "about me," Subscription spotlight, all included benefits, pricing, a preview of a sub-only videos and more, and subscribe from there. You can also preview your Subscription detail page by: Tapping your profile -> Subscription icon -> Subscription management page -> Subscription spotlight -> Preview (upper right corner) OR Going to self view of link on videos -> Subscription link

## Best practices and tips for creators

Simply adding links to your video isn’t enough — it’s the content that matters most! Here are 5 key things to remember to include in your video:


### 1. Tell your followers why they should subscribe

Show off your Subscription value with a compelling promo describing the awesome perks subscribers will have access to, like behind-the-paywall content, behind-the-scenes clips, extended video cuts, personal interaction with you through comments or DMs, or other benefits showcasing your unique personality. ### 2. Ask your audience for feedback

Encourage engagement and ensure your subscribers stay satisfied by directly asking what content they want to see or ways you might improve their experience. An easy way to do this is by using the poll sticker in combination with subscription links.


### 3. Entice viewers with your sub-only content

Share sneak previews of your sub-only content, tease future sub-only topics, and establish your sub-only content as an upgraded version of your regular content. It's all about making your community feel exclusive and exciting, so your audience can't wait to hit join!


### 4. Post consistently and share what's new

Keep your audience engaged by posting often — try once per week as a baseline. Make sure to include any updates to your benefits, pricing, recent sub-only videos or LIVEs, future content plans, etc. Or, consider asking your followers to help achieve a subscription goal in return for something of value!
