## What is the TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart?

The TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart is a weekly chart of the most popular songs on TikTok in the U.S. based on creations, video views, and user engagement. It is your window into the hottest songs on TikTok in the United States. These charts provide insight into the music that's resonating most with our creative community.


## Two Charts: Top 50 and Viral 50

There are two new trending music charts for you to explore: TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart: This showcases the most popular songs on TikTok, taking into account various signals and focused on local engagement. It's a true reflection of the current music trends in the U.S. market. What's more exciting? This chart is published both within the TikTok app and externally on Billboard. TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart Viral 50 Chart: This in-app exclusive chart highlights the fastest rising songs with global engagement. If you're looking to discover the daily viral music sensations, this chart is where you'll find them. Viral 50 Chart


## How to Find the Chart

Finding the TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart is a breeze: Open TikTok and go to any Sound Detail Page. Tap the top right button to access the charts page. Explore the TikTok Billboard Top 50 Chart and TikTok Viral 50 Chart to stay updated on the latest music trends.
