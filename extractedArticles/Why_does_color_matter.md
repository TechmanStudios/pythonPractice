## Understanding color theory

The color wheel can help you figure out color combinations that work well together and fit your vibe — think bold, high-contrast videos that scream high-energy vs. a cool, calming feel perfect for a more meditative piece of content.


### Complementary colors

Complementary colors are colors that are opposite each other on the color wheel. These colors create a high level of contrast when used next to each other and can help make your content feel super bright or vibrant!


### Analogous colors

Analogous colors are colors that are next to each other on the color wheel. These are colors that create a cohesive, harmonious feel when used together. You can play with brightness or saturation for these colors to keep things visually interesting!


### Warm and cool colors

Warm colors are colors that are associated with warmth, of course! This includes red, orange, and yellow. Cool colors are colors that evoke a sense of coolness, such as blue, green, and violet.
