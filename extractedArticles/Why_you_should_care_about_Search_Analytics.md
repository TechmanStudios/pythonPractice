## Key search metrics and what they mean

Click on the Search Analytics banner to uncover the5 most important search metrics. Tap the "i" to see their definitions. 5 most important search metrics Search Views: This tells you the number of times your posts were viewed on the search results page. When you see an arrow pointing up, it means this metric has increased compared to a similar period in the past. That's a great sign your videos are getting found in search! If the arrow is pointing down, it means your search views have decreased compared to a similar time period in the past. Check out the search friendly content tips in the next section to explore ideas for improving your performance in search results. Search Views Search View Percentage: This is the percentage of total views that came from the search results page and search feed. The higher this number, the more search-friendly your content is for the time period selected. Search View Percentage Search Impressions: This shows how many times your video appeared in search results, even if it wasn’t clicked. When impressions trend upward, that's a good indication that you’re using effective keywords or trending topics. Search Impressions Average Click-Through Rate (CTR): This is the percentage of impressions from the search results page that resulted in a click. A higher CTR means your video is compelling and relevant to the search terms. CTR = search views/search impressions. Average Click-Through Rate (CTR) Average Search Ranking: This is your video's position on the search results page. A higher ranking means your content is better positioned for clicks.
