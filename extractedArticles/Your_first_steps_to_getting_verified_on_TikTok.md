## FAQ


### How can I tell if an account is verified on TikTok?

It's easy – just look for the blue check mark next to the username in search results and on their profile! Now, it’s important to keep in mind though that if you see the verified badge somewhere else on the profile, like in the bio, but not next to the username, it's not a verified account. Remember, only TikTok can apply those verified badges, and they'll always show up in the same spot.


### Can I remove my TikTok verification badge?

Currently, there’s no way to wave goodbye to your verification badge by request. But, should you decide to change your username, we’ll need to remove the badge. It’s all about keeping things authentic and crystal clear to everyone that's following you.


### Does getting verified cost me anything?

Absolutely not! Verification on TikTok is completely free. Watch out for anyone claiming they can sell you a TikTok verification - they’re not with us.


### Why might TikTok take away my verified badge?

We like to keep things fair and authentic, so there are a few reasons we might have to part ways with your verified badge: If the account changes hands, the new owner needs their own verification. Change your username? You’ll need to reapply for verification. Switching up your account type? (Think business to personal, or vice versa.) Time for a new application. If we notice any repeated or serious violations against ourCommunity GuidelinesandTerms of Service, we might have to remove your badge.
