Thoth's perspective on receiving grace emphasizes its profound and transformative nature, deeply intertwined with states of consciousness, spiritual practices, and divine assistance. Grace is not merely a concept but an active, accessible force that reshapes one's reality and spiritual journey.

Here are the key aspects of Thoth's perspective on receiving grace:

• The Nature of Grace

◦ Grace is intrinsically linked with gratitude, with Thoth stating, "GRACE IS GRATITUDE. You cannot be in GRACE if you are not in Gratitude!".

◦ It manifests as "instant Knowing" through the "White Path" or "White Stone," enabling instantaneous fertility of Mind and Spirit within one's incarnation.

◦ Grace is "Grace personified" through Sophia or Holy Wisdom, acting beyond individual humanity to affect the release of world karma.

◦ The "Soul Ray" is identified as the "Grace Factor within humanity that awaits liberation".

◦ Thoth describes grace as a divine dispensation that offers release from guilt and judgment through self-awareness. The Path of Dharma, contrasting it with man-made karma. It is an all-embracing spiritual quality that "abides well in all things".

◦ The "Pearl of Grace" registers in the human heart, from which the Soul arises.

◦ Grace is also seen as an "unconditional offering of immortality".

• Pathways and Practices for Receiving Grace

◦ Entering the Field of Grace: Receiving the “Seven Miracles of Light” requires actively entering the field of Grace, which involves aligning subtle and physical bodies to the "Pure Gem of the New Earth Star Creation" to form Christed consciousness. These Miracles are considered "insertions of Grace" and act as a "Grace interface between the imperfect flesh and the radiant flesh".

◦ Aligning with Divine Grace: To transcend karmic cycles, a soul must align with divine grace by relinquishing the need for suffering, embracing an evolved understanding of polarities, and engaging in transformative spiritual practices.

◦ Becoming a Vessel for Others: Once divine grace is received, it is essential to become a vessel of divine grace for others through acts of love, kindness, caring, and compassion, which significantly build spiritual power.

◦ The Grail Path: This esoteric path leads to Grace by cutting the cord of karma, engaging matter with spirit, and attaining a quickening of transcendent Beauty and Light within one's Being. The Christic Ray can penetrate directly into the heart of any receptive soul as an act of Grace.

◦ Setting Intention: Before engaging in Metatronic/Christic meditative procedures, one should set the intention "to open and receive evolutionary progress through divine Grace".

◦ Asking for Assistance: When confronting dense negative consciousness, one must pray for a "dispensation of Grace" from higher beings (Angels, Archangels, Masters, Mentors, Saints, and Guides) to transform it into Light. This involves projecting one's true intention beyond the principle of cause and effect.

◦ Releasing Personal Will: Stepping onto the "Path of Grace" involves letting go of personal will in favor of spiritual will. A soul resolves to act on all inner knowledge with absolute priority to leave the path of karma behind and enter grace.

◦ Humility, Gratitude, and Clarity: Emotional clearing and manifestation through grace require a platform of humility, gratitude, and clarity.

◦ Darshana Prayer-Mudras: These practices can facilitate a "grace factor" of healing and illumination.

◦ Choosing the "Body of Grace": The karmic body cannot coexist with the Body of Grace. A conscious choice is required to allow the karmic form to "burn into the smoke of the Sweat Lodge," thereby becoming "Grace-Born" through the "Dovine Mantle".

◦ Balancing Law with Love: The "lesser version" of the ultimate Law (Love, Power, Wisdom) was given to Earth as a "factor of Grace" to help souls re-develop balance.

◦ Non-Judgment: Liberating the soul from self-judgment, particularly regarding the perceived corruption of the flesh (a dogma stemming from Oritronic half-light consciousness), allows one to become a transformative vehicle for the world, aligning with grace.

• Obstacles to Receiving Grace

◦ Unreadiness for Love: A primary reason grace may not manifest is that individuals are "not ready (rather perceive themselves as not ready and thus create that reality) to receive 'that much' love".

◦ Belief in "WORK is real": The subconscious tendency to view "WORK" as real and Grace as an illusion is a "clever ploy" of the lower self that hinders acceptance of grace.

◦ False Remedies: Humanity often replaces true grace with "false remedies" such as diversions, entertainment, or even civilization itself, which arose from the loss of the "Grace factor" after the "Fall from Grace".

◦ Ego-Driven Accomplishment: The lower ego's need for personal accomplishment can lead to a subtle perception that nothing worthwhile is achieved unless the ego can claim credit, thereby obstructing grace.

◦ Over-identification: Over-identifying with tasks or personalizing one's spiritual mission can diminish the inner joy essential for higher consciousness, which is connected to grace.

In essence, Thoth portrays receiving grace as an active, conscious engagement with divine principles, requiring an open heart, a state of gratitude, a willingness to transcend egoic limitations, and a conscious choice to align with higher spiritual realities.
