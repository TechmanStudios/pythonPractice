**The H Marker**

**Origin and Nature**

The H Marker is described as a **natural marker of sonic loci** (the power to position
consciousness) found specifically within the **“X” chromosome**.

**Purpose and Function**

• **Universal Role:** The H Marker acts as a **universal bridge and stabilizer for cosmic forces**
in various states.

• **Generation of Fields:** It is a **necessary frequency generator in the creation of
morphogenetic fields**.

• **Quantum Dynamics:** As a stable marker, it works in the **stimulation and direction of
powerful universal energy fields** by marking or tagging the **electron possibilities in quantum
dynamics**. This action forms **“dedicated infinity zones”**.

• **Higher Connection:** When working with Stargates, individuals are **entangling with high
Metatronic H Markers**, which assists in creating the necessary fields of FULL LIGHT on Earth for
the development of the Pyramids Radius Matrix (the New Earth launching platform).

stable fields **collapse**, but they can be manipulated immediately before collapse, performing a
sort of “puppet dance indefinitely”. This manipulation allows the H Marker’s sonic spin to be
changed to serve “another master”. Furthermore, the offspring of these manipulated markers
continue the “same puppet dance,” having been birthed in warped fields.

**The Reverse\-H (r\-H) Marker**

**Origin and Purpose**

The reverse\-H Marker (r\-H) was created to counteract the sonic violation and manipulation of the
natural H Marker. It functions as a **protective sonic wave**.

**Mechanism of Action**

• **Correctional Spin:** The r\-H works by **reversing the sonic spin** of the H Marker within the
specific parameters where the H Marker has been violated.

• **Selectivity:** It is important to note that the r\-H **has no effect on the balanced H**. It
only targets areas where the H is violated and is not operating correctly (”not playing its proper
tune”).

• **Destruction of Imbalance:** The r\-H serves to organize sonic fields generally, but its
primary corrective action is to **burst the bubble of imbalanced H spins** and **collapse the
vampirised version**.

• **Genetic Stabilization:** When an individual focuses or “entangles” with the quantum
generations of the Reverse H, they are actively **stabilizing all X chromosomes** by bursting the
decaying H Markers and initiating the birth of new, stable ones. The use of the r\-H also helps
inhibit the propagation of these “mutant H Markers”.
