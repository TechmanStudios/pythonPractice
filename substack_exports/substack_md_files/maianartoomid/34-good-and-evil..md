**Here is what Thoth has to say on the overcoming of evil from the [ThothStream knowledge base](https://nesialibraryproject.wordpress.com/thothstream-knowledge-base/):**

* **Thoth’s frame:** “Evil” isn’t an ultimate force; it’s a function inside Earth’s *dualistic realm* where some beings play the role of “Doers of Evil” within the evolutionary story. The aim is not endless combat but **transmutation and balance** back into the original pattern of Light.

* **Why good prevails:** We’re entering a **Period of Reclamation**—the Light “reclaims that which is rightfully its possession.” The Hierarchy *can only act through us* as we dissolve world\-karma and receive Grace; when we do, Light advances and the dark recedes.

* **How it happens (not by force but by alignment):** The **Metatronic\-Christic codes** realign humanity to the true pattern, dissolving distorted overlays set by Luciferic agendas. This “tunes” consciousness back to the authentic template, enabling Light to carry the field.

* **Outcome in the planetary story:** The world bifurcates into “**Two Worlds**.” Those released from mere cause\-and\-effect are *lifted* into the Coming Light, while the other stream “hides in darkness” and passes away. Good “prevails” as reality sorts by resonance with Light.

* **Practice cue:** Keep the current flowing **White → Red** (Spirit into matter), not the other way around. This is how Grace enters the blood\-path of life and transforms it—Thoth’s practical map for prevailing of the good in daily action.
