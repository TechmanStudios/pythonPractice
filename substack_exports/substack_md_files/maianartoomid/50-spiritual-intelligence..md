confuse, polarize and divide. We see it also in the spiritual “New Age” genre. There are videos,
etc with thousands of viewers spewing a potpourri of scary intrigue into their listeners ears.

Yes, the world is growing increasingly challenging and alarming in many respects. However, bear in
mind that the “archonically invested” (as Thoth calls all entities both human and non\-human who
serve the [Archonic Plasma
Cloud](https://nesialibraryproject.wordpress.com/akashic-definitions/archon-archonic-cloud/)) use
the truth to tell a lie. They do this to control the narrative in whatever genre humanity engages
which the “controllers” feel of importance to their agendas.

I wish to make it clear that it is not so much about “who do you trust” as a source as it is
about *trusting your own spiritual intelligence.* Yet first you must access that divine receipt
within. It IS available to you. You are a powerful spiritual being. You already know the
“truth.” It is up to you to move past believe systems, personal preferences and fears in order
to access it.

Spiritual Intelligence is brought forth from the *heart*\-brain and the *head*\-brain working
together as a balanced wave form.

***Now more than ever in this planetary experience, we are being called upon to become true Wisdom Beings instead of wisdom seekers.***

There are some very cleverly designed mis\-information nuggets being placed on the New Age table at
this time.

One is that when you die, you must not go into the Light as then your soul with be eaten by bad ETs.

Another is saying the higher Councils of Light have been compromised.

On the opposite side of the spectrum, is the message that all the bad ETs have been thrown out so we
are just fine now.

I could go on…and on. All patently false.

Some of this cacophony has truth embedded within it to lure the Seeker down the Rabbit Hole.

Heavy egos are making themselves (most unknowingly) available to the whispers of the OWL (One World
Legion serving the negative agenda), who set the mind traps.

It is not being suggested here that you close yourself off from all sources in fear that you may be
mis\-lead. Then you would find yourself in a box. Humanity learns and awakens through the process of
synergic relationships with one another.

*Live in the world but not of it.* 

Find your place through the spiritual intelligence that is an inherent gift within your soul.

Know that all these negative experiences, including the truth about the Greys, etc…ALL this is
operating on a very narrow yet highly magnetic bandwidth. It reminds me of the Wizard of OZ making
himself LARGE with his shadow. This is not to infer that it is not dangerous, but it is not as it
appears. Such intrusions can and *will* be overcome by Light Bearers raising their frequency as a
unified spiritual force.
