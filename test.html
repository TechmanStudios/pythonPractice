Using the TikTok app
Finding your way around TikTok is a breeze. Let's take a quick tour to help you get familiar with the app:
Home
Your home page is where the magic happens! Explore new content, interact with the accounts you follow, and search for topics that interest you. Here are all the features you'll find on your home page:
    For You:Your personalized feed delivers videos tailored to your interests. Like what you see? Engage with other creators by following accounts or liking and commenting on posts to helpthe recommendation systemselect content specifically for you. Not interested in a video you're seeing? Long press the video and click the "Not interested" button, and we'll show you less of that type of content in the future.
    Shop:Browse through shoppable content, explore popular products, and place orders, all within the TikTok app! As a creator, you can useTikTok Shopto recommend products andcollect rewards.
    Following:Curate your own feed by following your favorite creators and scrolling solely through their content. Start tobuild your communityon TikTok by connecting with fellow creators within your niche.
    Explore:Check out content currently making a splash with different audiences across the app! You can use the Explore tab to check out videos that have received high engagement, like likes, comments, shares, and views, but might not have made it to your For You feed.
    Search: Look up any topics to find top videos, sounds, accounts, content inspiration, and more! Use theCreator Search Insightstool to boost your traffic and engagement by staying on top of trends and discovering content gaps.
    LIVE: Watch creators express themselves and show off their creativity in real time. Learn from the creators who captivate you and considerbuilding your LIVE strategy!
Friends
If you follow an account and they follow you back, you're friends! In this section, scroll through and interact with just your friends' content.
Inbox
Stay in the loop with notifications for likes, comments, follows, direct messages, duets, and stitches. Don't worry— if you're not into notifications, you can adjust your settings.
Profile
This is your one-stop shop for everything about you—your profile pic, bio, followers, following, and the videos you've posted.Personalize your profileto show people who you are!
Create
Ready to unleash your creativity? Hit the plus icon to start creating! Choose your video length, play with creative effects, pick a sound, and let your imagination run wild. Remember, practice makes perfect, so don't be afraid to experiment and have fun with it!
